# Reviewer and Tester Instructions

1. Install Brightspace Dark+ from the Chrome Web Store or load the unpacked extension folder.
2. Open an HTTPS Brightspace environment supplied by your institution.
3. Click the Brightspace Dark+ toolbar icon. Confirm the popup reports **Brightspace detected** and select **Enable on this Brightspace site**.
4. Approve Chrome's prompt for the displayed school domain only.
5. Confirm that the current Brightspace shell changes to a dark background with readable navigation and controls. Reload once before checking embedded frames.
6. Close and reopen the tab to confirm the theme now runs automatically on the enabled school domain.
7. Switch between course filters or terms and confirm newly loaded course cards remain dark.
8. Open the calendar and confirm its grid stays dark while text on coloured events remains black and readable.
9. Open a course's table of contents and confirm selected rows, gradients, loading indicators, and navigation arrows remain readable.
10. Open the popup again, select **Disable on this site**, reload, and confirm the theme no longer runs on that domain.
11. On an unrelated HTTPS page, open the popup and confirm it reports **Brightspace not detected** without requesting site access.

The extension has no account, analytics, storage, network requests, or external service dependency. Do not provide personal Brightspace credentials to reviewers or testers. Institution-specific pages and branding vary; the completed live visual regression was performed on Carleton University's Brightspace environment.
